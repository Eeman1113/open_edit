On-device AI models used by the editor (loaded lazily from this folder).

migan_pipeline_v2.onnx
  MI-GAN (Picsart AI Research), https://github.com/Picsart-AI-Research/MI-GAN
  ONNX pipeline from https://huggingface.co/andraniksargsyan/migan. License: MIT.

realesr-general-x4v3.fp32.onnx / realesr-general-x4v3.fp16.onnx
  Real-ESRGAN realesr-general-x4v3, https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN
  Copyright (c) 2021, Xintao Wang. License: BSD 3-Clause.
  Converted to ONNX with scripts/ai-convert-esrgan.py (weights unchanged; fp16 variant for WebGPU).

depth-anything-v2-small.int8.onnx
  Depth Anything V2 Small, https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2
  ONNX export from https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small (onnx/model_int8.onnx).
  License: Apache-2.0.

magic_touch.tflite
  MediaPipe Interactive Segmenter (MagicTouch), https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/interactive_segmenter
  License: Apache-2.0.
