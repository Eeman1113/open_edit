# Feature inventory

Every user-facing feature of Open_Edit, where it lives in the app and its status. The list follows the app's own
routes (`src/app/**/page.tsx`, `src/app/api/**/route.ts`) and the panels and tool pages inside them. "Flow" is the
end-to-end flow in `scripts/e2e/flows/` that exercises the feature (see [testing.md](testing.md)).

Status:

- **Works**: implemented and passing its end-to-end flow.
- **Works, limited**: works, with a limit that comes from the platform or from a service you have to configure;
  the reason is given.
